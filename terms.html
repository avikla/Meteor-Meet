Terms of Use & Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What this service is

WhenFree is a free scheduling tool that helps groups find a meeting time that works for everyone. No account or registration is required.

This service is provided free of charge and as-is by Avi Klayman / WhenFree.

What data we collect
  • The name you enter when joining a meeting (stored in the meeting record)
  • The time slots you mark as available (stored in the meeting record)
  • The event name, date range, and time range you set when creating a meeting
  • The organizer's email address, if provided — used only to send a confirmation with the meeting link
  • Email addresses entered when sending invitations or best-time summaries — used only to deliver those messages and not stored in the meeting record

No passwords or account credentials are collected. Anonymous usage statistics are collected via Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (see Third-party services below).

How data is stored

All meeting data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, a cloud database operated by Google LLC. Data is associated with a randomly generated meeting ID and is accessible to anyone who has the meeting link.

Meeting data is retained indefinitely unless manually deleted. There is currently no automatic expiry of meeting records.

How data is used
  • To display availability to other participants in the same meeting
  • To calculate and show the best meeting times
  • To allow real-time collaboration between participants

We do not sell or share your meeting data with third parties. Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics are collected via Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to help improve the service.

Cookies & local storage

The app uses sessionStorage (cleared when you close the tab) to remember your name within a browser session.

localStorage is used to save your language and theme preferences across sessions.

Third-party analytics services set persistent cookies: Google Analytics sets a _ga cookie (retained for 2 years) to count unique visitors; Microsoft Clarity sets cookies for session behavior analysis. These are analytics cookies, not advertising cookies.

Third-party services
  • Google Firebase / Firestore — database and real-time sync
  • Google Fonts — typography (Figtree, DM Sans)
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — anonymous usage statistics
  • Microsoft Clarity — session behavior analysis (heatmaps, recordings)
  • ZeptoMail (Zoho) — transactional email delivery when you send invitations or best-time summaries

Each of these services is governed by its own privacy policy. Meeting content data is never shared with analytics providers.
