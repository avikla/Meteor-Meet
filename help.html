How to Use WhenFree

1
Create your event

Give your meeting a name (e.g., "Team Lunch", "Project Kickoff"), choose the dates you're flexible on, set a time range, and tell us who's organizing. Check the box if you want to be notified when people respond.

2
Share the link with participants

Once your meeting is created, you'll get a unique link. Copy it to share directly, or use the email button to send invitations. Add multiple recipients by typing an email and pressing Enter - previously used addresses appear as suggestions. On mobile, tap the contacts icon to pick directly from your phone contacts.

3
Participants mark their availability

Each person who opens your link enters their name and clicks on the dates and times when they're free. The calendar shows the heat map of when most people are available — darker green means more people can meet then.

4
Find the best time

At the top of the page, you'll see "Best times" — the time slots ranked by how many people are free. Pick the slot that works best and schedule your meeting!

Tips

  • Use the light/dark theme toggle in the top right to match your preference.
  • You can create multiple meetings and share different links for different groups.
  • Click individual time slots to mark or unmark when you're available — no need to drag.
  • Share the link without creating an account — anyone can join with just their name.
  • After picking the best time, click "Add to Calendar" to save it to Google Calendar or Outlook with one click, or download an .ics file for Apple Calendar and other apps.
  • For recurring weekly meetings, choose "Days of the week" mode when creating your event to find a slot that works every week.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account? ▼

No! WhenFree works without accounts. The organizer just needs an email address to receive the meeting link. Participants only need to enter their name.

Is my data safe and private? ▼

Yes. Your meeting data is stored securely in Firebase. Only people with the meeting link can view it. We don't sell or share your meeting data. Anonymous usage statistics are collected via Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to help improve the service.

Can I edit the meeting after creating it? ▼

You can edit the meeting name by clicking the pencil icon next to the title. To change dates or times, you'll need to create a new meeting. Participant responses are saved in real time, so you can check them anytime.

What if someone enters the wrong name? ▼

If someone rejoins with a slightly different spelling, WhenFree will recognize them as a returning user and ask if they want to continue as that person or use a new name. They can also clear their times and start fresh with a different name.

How long are meetings stored? ▼

Meetings stay available as long as needed. You can bookmark the link and come back to check responses anytime. If you don't use a meeting, it will still be there — nothing expires automatically.

Can I send the "Best Times" summary to people? ▼

Yes! Click the "📊 Send best times" button in the sidebar to email a nicely formatted summary of the top time slots. Add multiple recipients by typing an email and pressing Enter - previously used addresses appear as suggestions. On mobile, tap the contacts icon to pick directly from your phone.

What languages does WhenFree support? ▼

WhenFree is available in English, French, and Hebrew. Switch languages anytime using the buttons at the top of the page. The meeting creator's language preference is used as the default for other participants.

What if I need to add more dates later? ▼

Create a new meeting with the additional dates and share a new link. You can use a similar name so people know it's related (e.g., "Team Lunch - Extra dates"). Responses in each meeting are tracked separately.

Is there a limit to how many people can join? ▼

No limit! WhenFree can handle meetings with dozens or hundreds of participants. The interface updates in real time as people mark their availability.

Will I be notified when people respond? ▼

If you checked "Email me when someone responds" when creating the meeting, you'll get one email after someone finishes marking their availability (we wait 120 seconds after their last change). You can disable this anytime by unchecking the box before creating a new meeting.

Is WhenFree free to use? ▼

Yes, completely free - no accounts, no subscriptions, and no hidden fees. WhenFree is an independent project with no premium tiers.

What is "days of the week" mode? ▼

When creating an event, you can pick specific calendar dates (e.g., "March 15, 16, 17") or recurring days of the week (e.g., "Monday, Wednesday, Friday"). Days-of-the-week mode is ideal for finding a weekly recurring slot - like a standing team meeting. Specific dates are better for one-time events.

Can I delete a meeting or remove a participant? ▼

Meetings can't be deleted from the app, but since only people with the link can access them, you can simply stop sharing it. A participant can clear their own responses by reopening the meeting, re-entering their name, and choosing "Clear my times."

Does WhenFree work on mobile? ▼

Yes - WhenFree is fully responsive on phones and tablets. Tap individual slots to mark your availability. When sending invitations or best-times summaries, tap the contacts icon to pick recipients directly from your phone's address book.
